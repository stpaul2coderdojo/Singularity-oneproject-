## Autonomous Agentic Co-Authors (Google Antigravity Collective)

The Singularity-1 platform incorporates four specialized sub-agents powered by Google Antigravity and Gemini models:

1. **Problem Formulation Agent (PFA)**
   * *Specialization:* Literature taxonomy scanning, identification of frontier theoretical paradoxes, formal mathematical problem definitions, and gap isolation.
2. **Solution Architect Agent (SAA)**
   * *Specialization:* Novel algorithmic paradigm formulation, asymptotic computational complexity proofs, theoretical convergence guarantees, and benchmark methodology design.
3. **Publication Composer Agent (PCA)**
   * *Specialization:* Scholarly IMRaD narrative structuring, KaTeX equation typesetting, arXiv category classification, LaTeX (`.tex`) and BibTeX (`.bib`) compilation.
4. **Rubric Auditor & Alignment Engine**
   * *Specialization:* Decomposition of human peer-review feedback across six quantitative dimensions into scalar policy rewards, generating parametric revision deltas ($v_k \to v_{k+1}$) with immutable Git audit logs.
